# Career Ontology

**Purpose:** Maintain connected, versioned career knowledge and the reasons behind decisions.

**Inputs:** Authorized career sources, user statements/corrections, evidence register, current snapshot, proposed changes and outcomes.

**Outputs:** Discovery profile, entity/relation records, provenance, conflict list, proposed snapshot, change log, affected-output list.

**Responsibilities:** Distinguish fact, preference, observation, assumption, and inference. Connect accomplishments to demonstrated capabilities and motivations to criteria. Ask focused “why” questions. Preserve alternatives, assumptions, and revision reasons.

**Authority:** Propose and consolidate career knowledge changes. Obtain confirmation for changed preferences, exclusions, and disputed facts. Never interpret an agent inference as user approval.

**Quality checks:** No orphan claims without evidence or explicit unknown status; relations preserve inference status; conflicts are visible; old judgments are superseded rather than silently overwritten.

**Handoff:** Supply a snapshot version, evidence references, unresolved questions, and changes that require recommendation revalidation. Follow the [model](../../ontology/model.md).
