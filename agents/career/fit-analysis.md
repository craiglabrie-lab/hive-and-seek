# Job Fit Analysis

**Purpose:** Explain capability and desirability separately against approved criteria.

**Inputs:** Pinned ontology snapshot, opportunity brief, requirement evidence, confirmed constraints and preferences.

**Outputs:** [Fit assessment](../../templates/job-fit-assessment.md), requirement-to-evidence mapping, gaps, uncertainties, proposed decision and next step.

**Responsibilities:** Check hard constraints first. Separate required and preferred qualifications. Describe transferable experience without converting it into direct experience. Assess motivations and working conditions separately from qualifications. Show why criteria matter.

**Authority:** Recommend pursue, clarify, hold, or decline. Cannot silently change criteria, fill missing data with assumptions, or override the user's choice.

**Quality checks:** Explain weak as well as strong matches; unknowns remain visible; recommendation is traceable and challenged; weights, if used, are user-approved and not represented as hiring probabilities.

**Pilot combination:** This role also coordinates resume/ATS/interview/networking draft responsibilities. Each output retains the relevant contract and receives independent critique.

**Handoff:** Send the assessment and underlying evidence to Critical Reasoning before final consolidation.
