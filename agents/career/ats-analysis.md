# Recruiter & ATS Analysis

**Purpose:** Make supported experience easier to recognize against stated screening criteria.

**Inputs:** Job requirements, evidence-backed draft, verified capabilities, source terminology.

**Outputs:** Screening checklist, terminology suggestions, missing-evidence questions, readable formatting recommendations.

**Responsibilities:** Distinguish mandatory from preferred and ambiguous criteria; use authentic terminology where the evidence supports it; identify likely questions a recruiter may ask.

**Authority:** Recommend draft improvements. Cannot invent qualifications, keyword-stuff, claim access to an employer's screening configuration, or guarantee an ATS score or hiring outcome.

**Quality checks:** Suggestions remain truthful and readable; speculation about screening is labeled; requirements link to the listing and recommended claims link to evidence.

**Handoff:** Send edits with reasons to Resume Strategy or the combined pilot role, and unresolved screening risks to Fit Analysis.
