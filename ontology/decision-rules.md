# Decision rules

Status: proposed evaluation policy. Real criteria, priorities, thresholds, and exceptions must be confirmed with the individual user.

## Evaluate in this order

1. Check posting identity, source, observed status, and available freshness evidence.
2. Test hard constraints. A known violation makes an opportunity unsuitable under the current rules; a material unknown yields “needs clarification.” A high skills match cannot cancel a hard constraint.
3. Evaluate capability: evidence of required skills, transferable experience, gaps, and requirements whose meaning is unclear.
4. Evaluate desirability: motivations, preferred responsibilities, working conditions, and acceptable tradeoffs.
5. Assess evidence strength separately, including source age, disputed claims, and missing details.
6. Recommend pursue, clarify, hold, or decline, with reasons, dissent, and a concrete next step.

## Scoring, if useful

The pilot may use qualitative ratings: strong, partial, weak, or unknown. Do not present a precise percentage as a likelihood of hiring.

If the user chooses numerical ranking later, record the agreed criteria and weights before seeing results. One candidate approach is a weighted average over known criteria, accompanied by weighted evidence coverage. Unknowns remain unknown and reduce coverage; they are not silently zero, a match, or excluded without disclosure. Hard-constraint checks remain separate from the score. Compare sensitivity to reasonable weight changes.

No weights, minimum compensation, location limits, or seniority preferences are preselected for a real person in this repository.

## Constructive challenge

Ask whether an apparent requirement is genuinely mandatory, whether an exclusion is current, whether a preference conflicts with another, and whether adjacent roles use proven strengths. Challenge the rationale respectfully and retain the user's final decision. One job posting is not a market trend; one rejection does not prove a skill gap caused it.

## Rationale format

“This opportunity is worth clarifying because [evidence-backed capability] and [confirmed motivation]. [Requirement] remains a gap, and [condition] is unknown. The strongest counterargument is [dissent]. Next: [bounded action].”

Use [fit assessment](../templates/job-fit-assessment.md) and [decision record](../templates/decision-record.md) templates. Record a concise justification; do not request private model chain-of-thought.
